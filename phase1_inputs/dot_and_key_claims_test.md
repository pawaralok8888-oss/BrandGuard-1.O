# Dot & Key Sunscreen: Claims and Legal (Test Upload)

Mock rules for testing. Dot & Key is a real brand; these values are placeholders.

## 1. Absolute protection claims (Critical)

Never use "100% protection", "100% of UV rays", "waterproof forever", "blocks all UV" or "no white cast guaranteed".

## 2. Medical claims (Critical)

Never say the product "prevents skin cancer", "treats sunburn", "cures" or "heals" skin conditions.

## 3. Reapplication (Warning)

Reapplication advice must match the approved usage fact: [PLACEHOLDER: interval]. Do not state a different interval in any post.

## 4. Required disclaimer (Warning)

Any sunscreen benefit claim on Instagram or Ad must include: "Results may vary. Individual skin types may respond differently."

## 5. Creator disclosure (Critical)

Paid creator posts must start with "#ad" or "Paid partnership".
